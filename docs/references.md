# References

Sites and sources the design and content of consiglio.fyi draw on. The research behind them is issue #4.

## The model for the home

- **[paco.me](https://paco.me)** (Paco Coursey): typographic minimalism, one line per item, a short manifesto instead of a job title. The home follows this pattern: name, manifesto, then short lists (now, clients, before, contact).

## Product engineering

- **[Refactoring](https://refactoring.fm)** (Luca Rossi): newsletter on engineering and product leadership, by an Italian former CTO. A reference for how to write about the role and the craft, and a model of a project built around a point of view — see its piece on [product engineers](https://refactoring.fm/p/product-engineers).
- **[What is a product engineer?](https://posthog.com/blog/what-is-a-product-engineer)** (PostHog): the definition behind the positioning — outcomes and impact before the exact implementation.
- **[anttiviljami.github.io](https://anttiviljami.github.io/)** (Viljami Kuosmanen): a product engineer's site with a direct headline, a separate resume and a downloadable checklist.
- **[chicoria.github.io](https://chicoria.github.io/)** (Adilson Chicória): a tagline built on the product lifecycle ("Conceive · Build · Launch · Automate") instead of a title.

## Writing as proof of work

[simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net), [jvns.ca](https://jvns.ca), [overreacted.io](https://overreacted.io), [joshwcomeau.com](https://www.joshwcomeau.com), [maggieappleton.com](https://maggieappleton.com) (a digital garden). They show thinking through regular writing; the site has no writing section.

## Formats

- [nownownow.com](https://nownownow.com): `/now` pages — what someone is doing at the moment. The home's "Now" list plays this role.
- [uses.tech](https://uses.tech): `/uses` pages — tools and setup.
- [Europass](https://europass.europa.eu): the European CV. `/cv/europass/` follows its classic two-column layout.

## Typography

EB Garamond for text, IBM Plex Mono for labels and metadata, both self-hosted. The Europass page uses Arial Narrow, the typeface of the classic template.
